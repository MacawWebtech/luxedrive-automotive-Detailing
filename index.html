Keep the finish
looking new

A detail is only as good as how the car is washed afterwards. These habits protect your paint correction and coating between visits.

Book a maintenance detail

  • Wait a week before the first wash

    Ceramic coatings keep curing for around seven days. Keep the car dry where you can and avoid soaps until then.

  • Hand wash with two buckets

    One bucket holds soapy water, the other rinses the mitt, so grit stays out of the wash and off your paint.

  • Use a pH-neutral shampoo

    Harsh detergents and degreasers strip wax and wear coatings down faster than normal driving does.

  • Skip automatic brush washes

    Spinning brushes hold dirt from every car before yours and put swirl marks straight back into corrected paint.

  • Remove bird droppings and sap quickly

    Both are acidic and can etch clear coat within days in strong sun. Soften with a quick detailer, then lift off with microfiber.
