CERAMIC
COATING

Overview

Our ceramic coating service applies a 9H hybrid ceramic layer that chemically bonds to your paint, creating a hydrophobic, UV-stable finish that lasts years, not months.

Benefits

Hydrophobic Finish

Water and contaminants bead and sheet off the surface.

UV Resistance

Protects against oxidation and paint fading.

Chemical Resistance

Resists etching from bird droppings, sap and road grime.

Enhanced Gloss

Deepens colour and clarity for a wet-look finish.

What's Included

  • Full paint decontamination & clay treatment
  • Single-stage machine correction
  • Two-layer ceramic coating application
  • Wheel & glass coating add-on available
  • Curing inspection under cross-lighting

Process

01

Inspection

Paint-depth reading and condition assessment.

02

Preparation

Decontamination wash and clay-bar treatment.

03

Correction

Machine polish to remove minor imperfections before coating.

04

Application

Ceramic coating applied panel-by-panel in a dust-free bay.

05

Curing

Infrared curing followed by a final quality inspection.
