OUR STORY

LuxeDrive Auto Studio began as a single-bay operation focused on one thing: paint correction done properly. Over a decade later, the same obsession with surface quality now runs through every service we offer — from a maintenance wash to a full ceramic coating installation.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

We treat inspection as seriously as correction. Nothing gets polished or coated until we understand the paint's actual condition — because protection applied over the wrong preparation doesn't last.
